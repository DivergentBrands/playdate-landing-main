### **FAQ**

**Is Playdate a hand sanitizer?**

No. Playdate is an alcohol-free hand \+ skin cleanser. It is not marketed as a sanitizer or disinfectant and does not replace thorough handwashing when soap and water are available.

**What is hypochlorous acid?**

Hypochlorous acid, or HOCl, is a weak acid that is also produced by immune cells as part of the body’s natural response. Playdate uses a stabilized topical form at a concentration of 0.02%, or 200 ppm.

**What are the ingredients?**

Water, salt and hypochlorous acid. That’s all.

**Is Playdate made for sensitive skin?**

Playdate is fragrance-free, alcohol-free, pH-balanced, dermatologist tested and formulated with sensitive skin in mind. As with any skincare product, discontinue use if irritation occurs and follow all directions on the bottle.

**Where can I use it?**

Playdate is intended for external use on hands and skin. Follow the final directions printed on the product label.

**Does this replace soap and water?**

No. Soap and water remain the best choice for thorough handwashing and for visibly dirty, sticky or greasy hands. Playdate is designed for convenient, everyday cleansing when a sink isn’t nearby.
